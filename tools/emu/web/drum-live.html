ChoralRoot+ / Live Drums

Experimental browser audio prototype. This is not the firmware emulator or an installable FM1 build. Test the live mute, variation, fill and bridge workflow here.

Drum Live

Stopped · Variation A

Tap a drum part to mute/unmute. Tap FILL for a one-bar fill; hold FILL to repeat fills. Tap BRIDGE for a one-bar breakdown. Tap VAR to switch A/B at the next bar. All mutes persist through transitions.

Keyboard shortcuts

1 Kick · 2 Snare · 3 Hats · 4 Perc · 5 Variation · 6 Fill · 7 Bridge · Space Play/Stop

Audio starts after pressing Play (browser audio permission). This demo uses Web Audio drum synthesis, not ChoralRoot's embedded DSP.
